Load balancing demo

These buttons send real requests to /lab/api/whoami through Nginx. Each answer says which backend handled it. You change the settings in your Nginx config and your terminals, then come back here to see the effect.

Experiments to try:

1. Without zone in the upstream, send 10 in parallel: is the alternation strict?

2. Add zone backend_servers 64k; to the upstream, run sudo systemctl reload nginx, try again.

3. Stop the :3001 backend (Ctrl+C in its terminal) and send more requests.
